GET TO KNOW ME

I’m Caitlin.
From Trinidad and Tobago.

From a young age, I’ve had a deep curiosity about mathematics, physics, and space travel. Today, I’m pursuing separate B.S. degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Florida.

I’m fascinated by aircraft, rockets, satellites, and cars. My studies and project work give me opportunities to explore those interests through design, electronics, manufacturing, and research.

I expect to graduate in December 2028. In the meantime, my days at UF bring together rocket-team work, research, helping Statics students, and community leadership.

WHAT I’M DOING NOW

My current projects.

With Swamp Launch, I’m leading the mechanical design and integration of a CubeSat sensor suite, including mounts and sliders that allow sensors to deploy through the payload’s slots. I’m also developing the load-cell integration and verification approach for a rocket-motor test stand.

Previously, as NASA USLI Payload Electronics Associate Lead, I designed and printed electronics sleds, built PCBs, integrated sensors, and led soldering workshops.

RESEARCH & TEACHING

Research & teaching.

In Dr. Simmons’ research group, I’m studying “verification before reliance”: how engineers check AI-generated information before incorporating it into their work and decisions. I review research across engineering, AI, and human factors, and help turn those findings into research summaries and concept maps.

As a student assistant for Engineering Mechanics: Statics, I also help students understand solution methods and engineering principles. My responsibilities include reviewing calculations, units, and reasoning, and answering questions about course material.

BEYOND MY ENGINEERING WORK

Community is part
of my college life, too.

I serve as External Vice President of UF’s Indo-Caribbean Student Association, after previously serving as Social Chair. I help coordinate outreach and partnerships with campus and community organizations. As Social Chair, I planned cultural and social events and managed logistics.

I’m also a Lead Validator for Science CommuniGator, where I review student-written science articles for factual accuracy and source quality.

LOOKING AHEAD

My next chapter
in engineering.

I’m interested in beginning my career in aviation or aerospace design. I’m looking for engineering internships and job opportunities where I can contribute my experience in mechanical design, electronics integration, and manufacturing while continuing to develop as an engineer.

Over time, I’d like to explore a path into engineering management, helping guide technical work and the teams behind it. For now, I’m focused on developing my technical skills through project work and internships.
