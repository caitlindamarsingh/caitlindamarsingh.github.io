PROJECT 05 / Test hardware · Mechanical design

Developing static-fire test hardware.

I have worked on load-cell integration for our static-fire test stand and am now working on its modular, foldable blast shield.

ROLETooling Development Responsible Engineer
CONTEXTSept. 2026–present
STATUSOngoing design
IN THIS PROJECTThe problemMy roleDesign processResults & statusProject gallery

01 / THE PROBLEM

Design requirements.

Our team needs a test stand that transfers motor thrust to a load cell, along with a protective barrier for static-fire testing.

02 / MY ROLE

My contribution.

I led load-cell selection and helped design the test stand’s sensor mounting and force-transfer system. I’m now working on a modular, foldable blast shield for the stand.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

My design process.

  1. 01

    Integrate thrust measurement

    I worked on load-cell selection, sensor mounting, and the force-transfer arrangement within the team’s test stand.

  2. 02

    Develop the blast shield

    I’m working on a modular frame and shield arrangement that can be assembled, folded, and stored.

  3. 03

    Coordinate test hardware

    I’m continuing the tooling work across the test stand and blast shield, keeping their interfaces and handling requirements in view.

Tools & materials

  • Fusion 360
  • CAD assemblies
  • Load-cell selection
  • Sensor mounting
  • Modular frame design

Design challenges

The work brings together thrust measurement, structural interfaces, and a barrier that can be assembled and stored. Protective performance still requires verification.

04 / RESULTS & STATUS

Results & status.

Load-cell integration work has informed the test-stand design. Blast-shield development is ongoing; these CAD views show the design rather than a verified protective rating or completed test result.
