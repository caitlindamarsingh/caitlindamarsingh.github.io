PROJECT 03 / Rocket design · Simulation

Designing toward a 2,000-foot flight.

I evaluated a preliminary rocket design targeting a 2,000-foot apogee and parachute recovery.

ROLEPreliminary design evaluation
CONTEXTPreliminary design review
STATUSDesign & simulation

01 / THE PROBLEM

Design requirements.

I evaluated a single-stage rocket concept for a stable flight to approximately 2,000 feet and recovery by parachute, as part of my preparation for Level 1 high-power rocketry certification.

02 / MY ROLE

My contribution.

I evaluated vehicle sizing, material options, motor selection, recovery, and predicted flight performance for Luna V1. My Level 1 certification is in progress.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

My design process.

  1. 01

    Define the targets

    I used a 2,000-foot apogee target and a nominal 15 ft/s descent target to guide the design evaluation.

  2. 02

    Compare materials

    I compared strength, impact resistance, cost, and density using weighted decision matrices. The selected materials include Blue Tube, birch plywood, and a polypropylene nose cone.

  3. 03

    Assess predicted performance

    I assessed OpenRocket predictions for altitude, stability, and recovery against the design targets.

Tools & materials

  • OpenRocket
  • Material decision matrices
  • Blue Tube
  • Birch plywood
  • Nylon parachute

Design challenges

I weighed stability and structural requirements against mass, cost, and recovery speed. Material selection was a key part of balancing those competing needs.

04 / RESULTS & STATUS

Results & status.
