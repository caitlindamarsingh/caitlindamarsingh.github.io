PROJECT 01 / Electronics · Mechanical integration

Packaging electronics for flight.

I worked on electronics packaging, custom PCBs, and sensor integration for Swamp Launch’s NASA USLI payload.

ROLEPayload Electronics Associate Lead
CONTEXTMay 2025 – June 2026
STATUSDesign & fabrication
IN THIS PROJECTThe problemMy roleDesign processResults & statusProject gallery

01 / THE PROBLEM

Design requirements.

I needed to secure and organize the payload electronics while coordinating their placement with the mechanical structure and software interfaces.

02 / MY ROLE

My contribution.

I designed and 3D-printed three electronics sleds and their mounting hardware, built custom PCBs, integrated sensors, and troubleshot hardware during payload testing. I also led soldering workshops and coordinated integration with the payload mechanics and software teams.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

My design process.

  1. 01

    Package the electronics

    I developed CAD sleds and mounting hardware to locate and secure components within the payload.

  2. 02

    Integrate the hardware

    I assembled and soldered electronics, integrated sensors, and coordinated component placement with mechanical and software teammates.

  3. 03

    Review and troubleshoot

    I worked through safety and design reviews and troubleshot hardware during payload testing.

Tools & materials

  • Fusion 360
  • Custom PCBs
  • 3D printing
  • Soldering
  • Sensor integration

Design challenges

I had to account for mounting locations and component placement while coordinating interfaces across the electronics, mechanics, and software teams.
